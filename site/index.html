Relayloom pays a small factory's suppliers only for what actually arrived.

Meta models read the bill and count the delivered goods. If they match, agents enter the bill into the books and a separate check reads it back. If not, nothing is entered, the owner decides, and an agent drafts the dispute to the supplier. Private numbers are masked before anything leaves the building, and no invoice text can change who gets paid.

The problem at a small factory

Paid for 40, got 34

An oil mill in Salem takes in tins, seed bags and cartons every day. The bill says 40 bags. Nobody counts the stack before the accountant enters the bill.

Rates drift

A carton billed at Rs 18.50 when the purchase order said Rs 16.00 looks fine on its own. It only shows up next to the order.

Bank change scams

A bill can carry a line asking to pay a new account. Here, payment details come only from the supplier record in the books.

How it works

  1. Intakewatches the bills folder
  2. ReaderMuse Glimmer reads the masked bill into a strict schema
  3. CounterSAM 3.1 counts the goods in the delivery photo
  4. Matchercode compares order, bill and count
  5. Clerkenters a matching bill in ERPNext
  6. Checkerreads it back with separate requests
  7. DisputerMuse Glimmer drafts, a browser agent files it
