The problem on a receiving dock

For the receiving and accounts payable team of a manufacturer or distributor that buys physical goods.

Bills describe what was sent

The bill says 50 cartons. The dock counted 46. A three way match only helps if someone counts, and nobody counts every pallet.

Deliveries arrive in pieces

One order can arrive in two deliveries and be billed once. Quantities have to add up per order, not per bill.

The same goods get billed twice

A repeat bill under a new number, a rate above the order, a line of hidden text asking to pay a new account. Each looks fine on its own.

How it works

  1. Intakewatches the bills folder
  2. ReaderMuse Glimmer reads the masked bill into a strict schema
  3. CounterSAM 3.1 counts every photo of every delivery
  4. Matchercode compares order, bill and goods as a running total
  5. Investigatorworks every mismatch with cited evidence
  6. Clerkenters a matching bill in ERPNext
  7. Checkerreads it back with separate requests
  8. DisputerMuse Glimmer drafts, a browser agent files it

The Investigator

On every mismatch an agent works before a person sees the case. It recounts from every photo, adds up all deliveries on the order, checks the order price and the last price paid, looks for a duplicate bill, and checks the supplier's past shortfalls. Every statement in its finding cites a record, a photo or a recount, and code checks that each citation exists before the finding is shown. Then it proposes an action and the policy decides.
